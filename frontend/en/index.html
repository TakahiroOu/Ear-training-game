Ear Training Game: free online ear training

Train your ear with real piano sounds, right in your browser. Listen to one to four notes and find them on the keyboard, learn to recognize intervals and chords, and compete with players worldwide or with your friends. It's free, works on phones and computers, and needs no download.

What you can practice

  • Note and chord recognition: hear 1, 2, 3 or 4 notes and find each one on the piano. Choose a key to practice only its notes and chords. A right note in the wrong octave counts as half right.
  • Interval and chord trainer: minor 2nd to octave, major / minor / diminished / augmented triads, seventh chords and inversions, played together or one by one.
  • Ranked play, anytime: 20 questions, the same for everyone; answer fast for more points and climb the weekly and all-time worldwide leaderboards.
  • Live games with friends: everyone hears the same question at the same moment; the fastest right answers score.
  • Class competitions: teachers can create a contest and share a join code with students.

Who it's for

Music students preparing for dictation and aural skills exams, instrumentalists and singers who want better relative pitch, and anyone who wants to play songs by ear or hear harmony more clearly.
