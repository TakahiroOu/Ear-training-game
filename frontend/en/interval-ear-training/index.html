Interval ear training

An interval is the distance between two pitches. Recognizing intervals by ear makes it much easier to write down melodies, play songs by ear and sing harmonies: it's the most basic ear training skill.

How to practice

  1. Start with a few. Begin with intervals that sound very different (major 3rd, perfect 5th, octave), then add more.
  2. Practice melodic and harmonic. Notes one after another sound different from notes together.
  3. Sing before you answer. Hear the lower note, sing the upper one, then check.
  4. Five to ten minutes a day is enough to get noticeably faster.
